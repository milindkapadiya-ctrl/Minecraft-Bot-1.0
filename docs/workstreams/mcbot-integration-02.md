## Approved contract delta

The canonical ActionRunner retains a private operation reservation across sequential stationary scanner actions, planning gaps, guarded movement, arrival waits, digging, inventory verification and cleanup. Trusted compiled collaborators receive a revocable scoped execution handle, never an owner string or raw controls. Standalone callers and overlapping children refuse without queuing. Existing stationary `runExclusive`, physical watchdogs, cleanup bounds and fault closure remain unchanged. A 32-second work budget preserves the previous maximum of 20 seconds travel plus 12 seconds collection; at most500ms cleanup grace is tracked separately and cannot release an unsettled physical child into reusable admission. Unrecoverable cleanup closes the canonical controller. Timers cannot preempt blocking JavaScript; checkpoints enforce the monotonic deadline before supported work.

Each prospective waypoint opens one stationary acquisition batch. At most three real scanner calls, with explicitly accounted camera actions, complete before sealing. A fixed bounded schedule replaces preliminary planning followed by more scans. The final camera is the exact last scanner's restored acquisition pose, verified before seal; original per-scan provenance is retained privately. Exact XYZ, identity, lifecycle, delivered changes, monitoring continuity and unexpected camera/motion remain guarded throughout. Conflict tombstones persist within the batch and no cells carry across walking.

Single-use now means one sealed batch consumed before one immediate synchronous navigation decision. That decision may compare at most four target-adjacent paths and four local probe candidates with existing planner limits. This explicitly supersedes NAV-06's historical proposal of exactly one pure planner invocation per scan. No caching, second decision, reopening, external redemption token or movement authority follows. Already-queued invalidations are delivered at a retained-ownership handoff before fresh guarded movement. Expected collection block removal occurs only after route authority is retired.

Acquisition starts before any contributing look. First waypoint uses an initial scan and, only if source footing/body/head coverage is absent, a floor look/scan. After movement, a target-biased look/scan and a fixed lane look/scan precede that same coverage-only floor criterion. There is no preliminary route selection. Maximum per waypoint:3 completed scans,3 explicit external looks (each1500ms); each scanner retains its existing bounded three-view camera work and restoration. Maximum route:3 guarded waypoints, each with fresh acquisition. The existing20s travel cancellation now reaches scans and looks directly; collection keeps its12s bound under the32s operation deadline. No bound was extended to mask a regression.

Every genuine ScanResult keeps its actual three views and acquisitions. The aggregate is private KnownCell data, never relabeled as a ScanResult. Sealing requires no pending acquisition; consumption marks the batch used before validation/computation and never restores it after failure. A sealed batch left across a microtask is invalidated, expressing immediate continuity rather than a universal TTL. A consumed batch may only finish its tested `setImmediate` invalidation handoff, then is disposed. The subsequent support look is a separate guarded execution action; it cannot redeem or extend route admission.

The batch verifies its final camera against the last scanner's strictly restored entry camera, including supported pinned-client quantization. Each permitted camera action is accounted individually, not exempted for the whole scan. Unexpected delivered camera/position changes stay latched even when coordinates return. Monitoring loss and same-owner activity outside batch acquisition also invalidate. Whole-operation ownership prevents competing supported actions, not world changes.
