# Current-view land terrain

`observeNearbyTerrain(bot, readBudget = 4096)` returns fresh copied `KnownCell[]` for the unchanged planner. It is read-only and has no action-runner integration, controls, camera turns, memory, entity targets or inventory access. This is an immediate-use snapshot, not a validated freshness/session contract: callers can retain stale arrays, and no stale-snapshot rejection is implemented.

## Bounds and visibility

Valid poses produce 245 unique integer cells: a 7x5x7 volume about the feet cell, Y from one below through three above. Cell centers must be within four blocks of the eyes, yaw difference at most 0.7 radians and pitch difference at most 0.8. An exactly vertical ray has undefined yaw and is checked against pitch/range/occlusion only; every nonvertical ray retains both angle limits. Out-of-view/range cells remain unknown without target-content reads. Invalid/nonfinite pose, pitch outside +/-pi/2, eye height outside (0,2], or coordinates outside the conservative planner-compatible margin (absolute X/Z <=29,999,996 and Y <=4,092) return an empty array.

Visibility uses the segment/AABB approach established in water-exit, with independent land implementation to leave paused recovery untouched. Closed voxel boxes include grazing intersections, including both sides of an integral eye boundary. Entries are ordered; tied neighbors are checked before reading the target. An opaque/unloaded/unresolved voxel stops that ray. A voxel unknown on its own center ray can legitimately be queried along another unobstructed ray; it still remains unknown in output unless its center visibility is established.

Only empty air/cave_air/void_air transmit sightlines. Fluids, vegetation, glass and partial blocks conservatively occlude. This geometry model does not simulate darkness or rendering. No names, state IDs, raw block objects or hidden identities escape.

The default/hard maximum is 4,096 `blockAt` attempts per call, counting repeated reads and exceptions. An optional integer budget from 0 through 4,096 can lower the cap. Exhaustion makes unresolved cells unknown and performs no further block reads; earlier certified cells can remain known. Invalid budgets return an empty array. Missing/malformed block data or read exceptions cannot certify safety.

## Classification and consumers

Classification reuses exported `support` and `clear` from `src/actions/step-up.ts`, after visibility is established:

- `support`: one exact full cube of grass_block, dirt, stone, cobblestone or planks under existing movement policy.
- `clear`: collision-free air/cave_air/void_air or wildflowers under that policy. Wildflowers can be clear themselves while conservatively occluding cells behind them.
- `blocked`: other observed geometry/material, including water/lava, sand/gravel and partial blocks. This certifies neither traversability nor material identity.
- `unknown`: visibility, loading, input validity or read budget is insufficient. Omitted cells also remain unknown to the planner.

The observer does not certify an entire standing footprint, route, braking buffer, physical safety or arrival. Those remain consumer checks. Center visibility can leave distant floor cubes, underfoot support and body/headroom cells unknown even on a flat floor. No ground contact inference or synthetic clearance fills those gaps. Will must review useful coverage and refresh/revalidation before production integration. ## Pure evidence merger — Task 6

`mergeObservations(input)` in `src/perception/merge-observations.ts` consumes data only. It imports existing `KnownCell`/position/terrain types, with no runtime bot, world, clock, controls or navigation imports. It acquires no views and retains nothing across calls. The original observer and Task 4 diagnostic are unchanged; a controlled acquisition adapter remains future work behind the Task 5 exclusivity gate.

Each perception-local `CapturedView` supplies `viewId`, `sessionId`, `scanId`, nonnegative safe-integer `capturedAtMs`, booleans `complete`/`stationary`, finite position/yaw/pitch, and `KnownCell[]`. IDs are 1-64 characters from letters/digits/underscore/dot/colon/hyphen. Exact record fields are required; extra raw block names/state IDs or hidden metadata are rejected. Pose bounds match the observer's conservative coordinate margins and pitch limits. Each cell must be an integer coordinate within that pose's 7x5x7 volume and use the existing terrain enum. Sparse lists are allowed: omissions remain omissions and mean unknown to a downstream supplied-cell consumer.

All input views must declare complete, stationary acquisition in the same session AND scan. Their positions must match exactly, without drift tolerance; camera yaw/pitch may differ. View IDs must be unique; capture times must be nondecreasing in the supplied sequence. Equal timestamps are permitted for a coarse common clock and preserved as supplied. Missing metadata, invalid coordinates/records, declared incomplete/nonstationary captures, different sessions/scans, drift and reversed capture ordering reject the whole batch with an empty cell result. Failure codes are `invalid_input`, `limit_exceeded`, `inconsistent_views` or `incomplete_view`; no partial successful prefix escapes.

Bounds: 1-3 input views, at most 245 raw cells per view (duplicates count), explicit 735 total raw-cell limit, and at most 245 unique output coordinates within the identical stationary volume. The total limit also follows from the first two limits. Coordinate union is sorted numerically X/Y/Z; coordinates are never invented. Matching known classifications deduplicate. Unknown is absence of evidence and does not erase a known observation. Two differing known classifications invalidate the coordinate to unknown, even within one view; a later agreeing classification does not repair that conflict. Other uncontested coordinates can remain known. Empty/unknown-only compatible captures return `ok=true` with `hasKnownEvidence=false`, never a claim of useful coverage.

Success returns copied `cells`, per-view metadata in capture order, a first/last capture window, and coordinate-aligned provenance containing contributing `knownViewIds` and `conflict`. Unknown views do not become contributors; a conflicted coordinate records all contributing known-view IDs while remaining unknown. Returned cells, poses, metadata and ID arrays are independent of input data and later calls. `ok` means input consistency/merge success, NOT full terrain knowledge or physical safety. Per-view `complete` means declared acquisition completion, not an exhaustive map. Budget-limited snapshots can legitimately contain unknown cells; genuinely failed/incomplete acquisition must be declared incomplete by its future producer rather than passed as complete.

These fields are explicitly injected offline evidence, not production guarantees. The merger cannot prove visibility/occlusion, read-budget usage, continuous stationarity between captures, absence of block changes, authenticity of identifiers, common clock origin or current freshness.
